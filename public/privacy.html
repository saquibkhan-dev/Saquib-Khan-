Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what happens when you visit this personal portfolio site (the "Site"), operated by Saquib Khan.

What this Site does not do

This Site has no backend, no database, no login, and no contact form. It does not collect, store, or sell any personal information you submit, because there is nowhere for submitted data to go — the "Hire me" and contact links on this Site open your own email or phone app directly.

Cookies and local storage

The Site may store a small preference (such as your choice on the cookie/analytics consent banner, or a light/dark mode setting) in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled on this Site, it only loads after you accept the consent banner, and is configured to avoid collecting personally identifiable information where the analytics provider supports that option. You can decline the banner and continue using the Site normally.

Third-party fonts

This Site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading a font may cause your browser to make a request to Google's servers, which can see your IP address and the fact that this Site was visited, consistent with Google Fonts' own privacy practices.

Links to other sites

Project links on this Site (SprintDesk, CaveMart, and others) lead to separately hosted applications with their own privacy practices. This policy does not cover those destinations.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove any preference this Site has saved. If you have questions about data handling on this Site, contact saquibkhanjava@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Site changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
